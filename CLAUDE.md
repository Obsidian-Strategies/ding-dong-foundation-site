# The DingDong Foundation site

Public source for thedingdongfoundation.org. The full project brief, client facts, phase, and open questions live in the private repo one folder up (`../CLAUDE.md`). Read it when working from the parent folder. Follow the `client-website` skill.

## Rules that must hold (also in README.md)
- Nothing below 12pt (16px); body 20px (client, 2026-09-29). Every text/background pair at or above 4.5:1.
- Home copy is the client's verbatim text from her 2026-09-10 PDF. Her own edits of 2026-10-16 are applied (support/use, "determined individually and discussed after the first review", "without bells", "in voice"). The Lord's Prayer in full is the last section of Inspirations (client, 2026-10-16), white on the page navy, no band. The filed purpose statement is not on the site, and there is no Mission page or `/mission/` address.
- No founder name, bio, or photo credits anywhere. No email, contact link, or ask form anywhere; no Ave Maria dedication; footer is the round bell photo from her business card (`uploads/footer-bell.jpg`) beside the legal name, "A 501(c)(3) private foundation", and "Sarasota, Florida"; photos are the client's own or public domain, uncaptioned (client, 2026-09-29) except the Inspirations tower photo, captioned and outlined in gold at her request (2026-10-12, 2026-10-16).
- Give (client, 2026-09-29; Rogan the same day: humble, no bright button): check first, then online as an underlined line of text, "Give by card on Stripe" that goes to a Stripe Payment Link from `src/_data/site.json` (Stripe's hosted page; nothing entered here), a stub with a note until the link exists. Copy says the foundation covers the processing fee and a receipt is emailed automatically. The v2 amount-and-details mock is gone. By check is her 2026-10-16 wording verbatim, with P.O. Box 1414, Sarasota, FL 34230 and "A receipt will be sent."; the matching-gifts line and the tax line left on 2026-09-29 (Rogan).
- Guidelines and Apply (client emails, 2026-09-29; built 2026-10-10 on Rogan's instruction, replacing the proposal-first flow from the call notes of the same day): a short first inquiry, no documents yet. Guidelines is Who we fund; What a grant can pay for / What a grant cannot pay for (her carillon note under the first list); How to apply (her three steps in her 2026-10-16 wording, then "Grant amounts are discussed after the first review."). Apply is her seventeen fields in her order, date auto-filled, the amount requested optional (2026-10-16), plus an optional Documents field of up to ten files (Rogan, 2026-10-12); her "do not attach" line is gone. "Inquiry" and "first review" are her words; never "approve", "reject", or any invitation to write and ask. No percentage, no "proposal in hand". Form delivery is stubbed.
- Times New Roman everywhere except the motto, set in Great Vibes through `--font-script`: "Make a Joyful Noise to the Lord" on the entrance overlay; on Home, "Jubilate Deo" as the h1 with the English motto in script and "Psalm 100:1" under it, then the opening paragraph centred, larger, semi-bold, and gold notes drifting up the hero's right side (`_includes/home-notes.njk`, ours until her graphic arrives) (client, 2026-10-12 and 2026-10-16, reversing 2026-09-23). The wordmark is "The DingDong Foundation" in bold small capitals, as on her business card; stacked, "Foundation" is one size up and both lines share their edges (2026-10-16). The entrance overlay shows no name (client, 2026-09-29): bell, small motto, small hairline button. One dark theme, no toggle. The entrance overlay is the one light (ivory) surface. Sentence case except her title-case titles of 2026-10-16 ("Grant Guidelines", "Apply for a Grant", the three Resources trade headings); no page title ends in a period. No emoji, verb-phrase buttons.
- "Apply for a grant" is the last of six nav links, a plain link, and lives nowhere else (client, 2026-09-06 and 2026-09-29: top navigation only). No Apply button anywhere; the header is the wordmark left, six links right. Applications are online only: no paper form, no mailing option. Funded projects are shared on the site; applicants do not opt out.
- Every page has the same shape (2026-09-29): one 1200px column, plain h1 plus one white paragraph on the hero, h2 plus white `.section-p` paragraphs per section. No eyebrows, no italic words in headings, no cards around plain text, no hover lift on static content. Questions has no ask-your-own form.
- Banned strings in built HTML: February, Europe, Grounded, Judy, Peng, founder, Ding Dong, public charity.
- Pages: Home, Inspirations, Guidelines, Give, Resources, Apply (six nav links). Resources is makers in three trades (bells with U.S., electronic systems, abroad; organs; rose windows and stained glass), her lists of 2026-09-29, then the questions. The disclaimer sits once in the Resources hero. `/story/` redirects to `/inspirations/`; `/questions/` redirects to its section on `/resources/`.
- One text colour: white. Gold is the only accent. No pink (client, 2026-09-23). The footer carries "A 501(c)(3) private foundation". The Lord's Prayer is on Inspirations at 24px, drifting slowly through a ten-line window, paused on hover (no button), still under reduced motion. Grants is hidden (permalink false) until the first grant.
