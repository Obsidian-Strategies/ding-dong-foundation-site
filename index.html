What we fund

The sacred elements that give a place its voice, its light, and its craft.

  • Bell towers & bells

    Repair, recasting, and new bells for towers that have fallen silent.

  • Pipe organs

    Restoration and purchase of the instruments that carry a congregation's voice.

  • Rose & stained-glass windows

    Conserving the color and light of historic windows, and commissioning new ones.

  • Sacred arts & artisans

    Training the bell founders, organ builders, and glass artists who keep these traditions alive.

  • Sound, color & frequency

    Supporting spiritual organizations that use sound, color, and frequency for healing.

Our story

When our founder was a student in Europe, church bells rang every half hour — and all Sunday long. Back home in the States, they had mostly gone quiet. The DingDong Foundation began with a simple wish: to hear them ringing again, joyfully, as a call to prayer and a call to gather.

Founded in February 2026 as a Florida 501(c)(3), we fund the building and restoration of church bell towers, pipe organs, rose and stained-glass windows, and the sacred arts — and the artisans who keep them alive. We believe sacredness belongs in daily life, and that sound and color can heal.

Not a product — this is a gift.

How a grant works

Simple to start. Careful where it counts.

  1. Inquire online

    Tell us about your church or organization, the project, and what it needs. A short form is all it takes to begin.

  2. Preliminary review

    We read every inquiry. If the project fits our mission, we'll ask for supporting documents — quotes, photos, drawings, deeds.

  3. Board decision

    Our board votes on each project. Grants are sized to the need, case by case.

We fund 501(c)(3) organizations only, not individuals. Projects are typically Christian-based, though the board considers every worthy proposal.
